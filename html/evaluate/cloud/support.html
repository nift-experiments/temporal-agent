Priority definitions

  • P0 - Critical (Production impacted)
    • The Temporal Cloud service is unavailable or degraded with a significant impact.
  • P1 - High (Production issue)
    • An issue related to production workloads running on the Temporal Cloud service, or a significant project is blocked.
  • P2 - Normal (General issues)
    • General Temporal Cloud service or other issues where there is no production impact, or a workaround exists to mitigate the impact.
  • P3 - Low (General guidance)
    • Questions or an issue with the Temporal Cloud service that is not impacting system availability or functionality.
On Page Service

Temporal dedicated platform architect​

Mission Critical customers, and Enterprise customers who opt in, get a Dedicated Platform Architect:

  • A senior developer expert joins your Temporal account team, and because they already know your implementation, issues resolve faster.
    • Advisory on best practices, and development pairing on your code.
    • Regular reviews with recommendations to improve performance and efficiency.
    • Input on implementation design and operations.
  • Priority access to that engineer for up to 20 hours per month, for one business unit or major group within a single region.

Engineers are assigned in your local time zone. Buy additional service units to cover more groups or regions at $6,000/Mo/Unit. One unit of Mission Critical Support includes:

  • Up to 20 hours per month
  • One major group or business unit
  • Limited to one region
  • Quarterly onsite visits

Ticketing​

Temporal Cloud customers file tickets in the Temporal Support Portal. For questions outside a ticket, use the community Slack and the community Discourse forum.

info

The Temporal Support Portal is for Cloud customers only, and all Cloud customers pay for support as part of their plan. Other Temporal users have full community access except for the #support-cloud channel.

Access Temporal support​

  1. Go to support.temporal.io.
  2. If prompted, log in to Temporal Cloud with the method you normally use, such as Google, Microsoft, or email and password.
  3. View open and closed tickets for your Temporal account, or submit a new one.

To request assistance from Temporal Support, see Create a ticket.

Create a ticket​

info

This procedure applies only to Temporal Cloud customers whose contracts include paid support. Without paid support, post your request in the Temporal Community Forum or the #support-cloud channel of the Temporal workspace in Slack.

To create a ticket in the Temporal Support Portal:

  1. Go to support.temporal.io.
  2. If prompted, log in to Temporal Cloud using the same method you normally use (for example, Google, Microsoft, email-password, or other methods).
  3. Click the Create Ticket button in the top right corner.
  4. On the Submit a ticket page, enter the details of your request into the form. Name, Subject, and Description are required.
  5. At the bottom of the form, choose Submit.
